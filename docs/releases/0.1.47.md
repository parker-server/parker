# Parker 0.1.47 Release Notes

Status: Draft

## Added

- None yet.

## Changed

- Every page now picks a series cover with the same rule: the earliest volume's first regular issue, so #1 when that volume has one, and its lowest issue when it doesn't (Captain America's 1968 volume starts at #100, continuing Tales of Suspense). Annuals, specials, #0, negative and .5 issues are skipped unless nothing else exists, and known reverse-numbered series still use their highest issue. This was already the series page's rule; the library series list, the series list, Advanced Search's Series view, Home rails, Featured Rails, creator pages, OPDS feeds, and the series page's background image and colors now follow it too. Before, they each had their own rule.
- Creator pages pick each series' cover from the earliest volume of that creator's issues, instead of the earliest-dated #1.

## Fixed

- Fixed Advanced Search's Series view and Home rails sometimes showing a later volume's cover for a multi-volume series, such as Action Comics showing its 2011 #1 instead of its 1938 #1. They took whichever #1 the database returned first.
- Fixed the library series list, the series list and OPDS feeds showing a later volume's #1 when the first volume has no #1, such as Captain America showing its 1996 #1 instead of its 1968 #100.
- Fixed the series page's background image and colors coming from a different issue than its cover for a series whose first volume has no #1: Captain America showed its 1968 #100 as the cover but drew the background from volume 2 #1.
